# Stockholm Weekend: Sat 17 to Sun 18 Oct 2026

Saturday is a full day. Sunday ends around 16:00.
Mid-October: about 6–10 °C, sunset around 17:25, and showers are likely. Bring a layer.

**Getting around:** tap a contactless card or your phone on SL readers (metro, trams, buses and the Djurgården ferry all count). A single fare covers 75 minutes. Most of the centre is also walkable.

**Heads-up:** *dagens lunch*, the cheap weekday lunch deal, doesn't exist on weekends. The best cheap local food then is market halls, street food and bakeries.

---

## Saturday: Djurgården, then Södermalm

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:45 | **Fika breakfast at Vete-Katten** (Kungsgatan 55) | Classic konditori since 1928. Get a *kanelbulle* or *kardemummabulle* and coffee. Cheap and very Swedish. |
| 09:40 | Tram 7 from Sergels torg toward Djurgården (~15 min) | |
| 10:00 | **Vasa Museum** | A complete, salvaged 1628 warship. This is the one museum you shouldn't skip. Arrive at opening to beat the crowds; allow 1.5–2 h. |
| 12:00 | *Option A:* **Skansen** (open-air museum, Nordic animals, old Swedish houses), 1–1.5 h. *Option B:* **Nordiska museet** next door, if it's raining. | Skansen has shorter autumn hours, so go before lunch. |
| 13:30 | Djurgården ferry from Allmänna gränd to Slussen (~10 min) | Nice harbour views and covered by your SL fare. |
| 13:45 | **Lunch: Nystekt Strömming** (fried-herring cart at Södermalmstorg, by Slussen) | Fried Baltic herring with mash and lingonberries. Very local, about 100–150 SEK. If it's closed for weather, go to **Meatballs for the People** (Nytorgsgatan 30). |
| 14:45 | **Fotografiska** (Stadsgårdshamnen 22) | World-class photography museum in an old customs house. The top-floor café has one of the best views in the city, so it's a good place to sit for a bit. |
| 16:45 | Walk up to **Monteliusvägen** | A cliffside path with the classic view over Riddarfjärden and City Hall. Be there for sunset at about 17:25. |
| 17:45 | Wander SoFo (the streets around Nytorget): vintage shops, bars | |
| 19:00 | **Dinner: Kvarnen** (Tjärhovsgatan 4) *or* **Pelikan** (Blekingegatan 40) | Both are old-school Swedish beer halls. Order *köttbullar* (meatballs), *pyttipanna* or *wallenbergare*. Mid-priced but real local food. Book Pelikan ahead; it fills up on Saturdays. |
| Later | Drinks around Medborgarplatsen or Nytorget | |

## Sunday: laptop time, Gamla stan, out by 16:00

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | **Laptop and breakfast at Café Pascal** (Norrtullsgatan 4, Vasastan) | One of the best specialty coffee spots in town, with good pastries and a calm morning crowd. About 1.5–2 h of work. |
|  | *Backup work spot:* **Kulturhuset** (Sergels torg) | Free library and lounge floors with wifi, plugs and lots of seating. Good if Pascal is full or bans laptops at weekends. |
| 11:15 | Metro to Gamla stan | |
| 11:30 | **Gamla stan walk:** Stortorget, Mårten Trotzigs gränd (the narrowest alley), Storkyrkan, and the outside of the Royal Palace | The **Nobel Prize Museum** on Stortorget is a good 45-min indoor stop. The palace guard change happens around midday; check the time that morning. |
| 13:00 | **Lunch: Hermitage** (Stora Nygatan 11) | Cheap, good vegetarian buffet in Gamla stan. *More Swedish but pricier:* **Under Kastanjen** for meatballs or *toast skagen*. |
| 14:00 | Walk over the bridge to **Skeppsholmen**, then **Moderna Museet** | The permanent collection (Picasso, Dalí, Warhol, Swedish modernists) is free. Spend 45–60 min there, or just walk around the island for the harbour views. |
| 15:15 | **Last fika: Chokladkoppen** (Stortorget) *or* **Café Saturnus** (Östermalm, huge cinnamon buns) | |
| 15:45 | Head to T-Centralen | The Arlanda Express takes about 18 min. Allow time if you're flying out. |

## If you have spare time or it rains

- **City Hall (Stadshuset):** guided tours only, and the tower has great views. Book on the day.
- **Östermalms Saluhall** (Saturday only, closed Sunday): a beautiful food hall. Grab *räksmörgås* (shrimp sandwich) or herring.
- **Hötorgshallen** (Saturday only): cheaper food hall with good falafel and Swedish deli counters.
- **Fabrique** bakery (all over town): reliable cardamom buns for snacking on the way.
- **Fotografiska** stays open late, so it can move to the evening if Saturday afternoon runs over.

> Opening hours shift in autumn, so check Vasa, Skansen, Fotografiska and the herring cart on the morning you go.
